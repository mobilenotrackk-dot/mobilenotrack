ℹ️ About Mobile No Track

Mobile No Track is a free online tool that uses the libphonenumber-js library to show the validity, country, telecom circle (for India), and landline area-code city (for select countries) of any mobile/landline number.

This tool does not reveal a person's exact real-time location, address, or identity — it only provides region-level information based on publicly available numbering-plan data.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Data Usage: This website does not store the phone numbers you enter anywhere — all processing happens client-side in your browser.

Cookies and Advertising: This site uses Google AdSense. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services: This site uses OpenStreetMap for map data, and geojs.io / api.ipify.org for the IP location feature.

GDPR/CCPA: For more information, see Google's Ads Policy.

Utility tools: Files, images and text used in the QR, image, PDF and age tools are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. The QR tool loads its script from cdnjs.cloudflare.com; other scripts load from unpkg.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net.

Domain and IP tools: When you use DNS, WHOIS, IP or VPN checks, the domain or IP you type is sent to third-party services (dns.google, rdap.org, geojs.io, api.ipify.org) to get the result.

Third-party advertising cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and may place and read cookies or use web beacons in your browser.
