What Is Mobile Number Portability (MNP)? Complete India Guide (2026)

Switch telecom networks between Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, Vodafone Idea (Vi), and BSNL without changing your primary phone number. Here is how MNP works.

For millions of smartphone users across India, a mobile phone number is much more than a contact string — it is linked to bank accounts, Aadhaar credentials, UPI payment apps, and personal digital identities[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). Historically, changing telecom operators meant abandoning your existing number and manually notifying hundreds of contacts[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

Mobile Number Portability (MNP) completely eliminated this friction[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). Regulated by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI), MNP grants every Indian mobile subscriber the legal right to switch service providers — from Airtel to Jio, Jio to Vi, or Vi to BSNL — while retaining their exact 10-digit phone number[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

In Simple Words: MNP lets you keep your exact phone number while changing network operators[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). Your call routing, SMS delivery, and mobile data services shift to the new operator smoothly without changing the number printed on your business card[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

How MNP Works Behind the Scenes in India

When you initiate a porting request, your original operator (Donor Operator) and your chosen new provider (Recipient Operator) coordinate through a central database entity called the Mobile Number Portability Clearinghouse (MNPCH).

In India, two authorized agencies — Zone-1 (M/s Syniverse Technologies) and Zone-2 (M/s MNP Interconnection Telecom Solutions) — manage nationwide porting databases. When a port completes, the MNPCH updates national routing tables so incoming calls and SMS are redirected through the new operator's radio network towers instead of the original owner's architecture.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Port Your Mobile Number

Porting your mobile connection in India is a quick, regulated process[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span). Follow these four structured steps[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span):

1

Generate your Unique Porting Code (UPC)

From the mobile number you wish to port, open your SMS app and send: PORT [space] [your 10-digit mobile number] to 1900[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span). You will receive an automated reply containing an 8-character UPC code and its exact expiration timestamp[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

2

Visit the New Telecom Operator's Store

Go to an authorized store or executive agent of the network you wish to join (e.g., Jio Store, Airtel Express, Vi Shop, or BSNL Kendra)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span). Carry your UPC code, an original identity proof document (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Driving License), and a recent passport photograph[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

3

Complete Digital KYC and Pick a Plan

The store representative will execute Digital e-KYC using your Aadhaar biometrics or facial authentication. Select your prepaid recharge plan or postpaid tariff tier, and receive a new unactivated SIM card[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

4

Wait for the Network Migration Switch

Intra-circle porting takes roughly 3 working days[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span). You will receive an SMS notifying you of the exact porting time (typically scheduled overnight between 1:00 AM and 5:00 AM to minimize service disruption)[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span). Swap your old SIM for the new SIM when the old network stops displaying signal bars[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).

TRAI Eligibility Criteria & Porting Regulations (2026)

To prevent fraudulent porting and maintain telecom stability, TRAI mandates specific conditions before a UPC code can be issued[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span):

  • 90-Day Minimum Tenure: Your mobile number must be active on your current network for at least 90 days before you can request another port[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).
  • Clear Postpaid Bill Dues: Postpaid subscribers must clear all billed outstanding amounts with their current provider before initiating a port[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).
  • No Pending Legal Ownership Disputes: The number must not be subject to court orders or active law enforcement block requests.
  • 7-Day SIM Swap Cooling Period (Anti-Fraud Rule): If you recently replaced a damaged or lost SIM card, TRAI strictly prohibits UPC generation for 7 days post-replacement to prevent unauthorized takeover attacks.
  • UPC Code Validity: UPC codes are valid for 4 days in most Indian telecom circles (and 30 days in Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and North East circles)[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
⚠️ Balance Alert: When porting a prepaid connection, any remaining talktime balance, active daily data packs, or validity days on your current SIM will be forfeited permanently once the switch completes[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span). Time your porting request close to your recharge expiry date[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).

Why Mobile Lookup Tools Show the "Original Operator" After Porting

A frequent point of confusion among users searching numbers on tools like Mobile No Track is why a ported number still shows its old network provider[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span).

The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) divides India into 22 Licensed Service Areas (Telecom Circles) and allocates 10-digit number blocks according to the National Numbering Plan (NNP)[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span). For instance, a number block starting with 9810X was originally allocated to Airtel in the Delhi NCR circle[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).

Public lookup tools search against these official NNP allocation master tables[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span). Because real-time ported routing data (MNPCH) is restricted strictly to licensed operators for routing calls and billing, public lookup tools can only accurately reflect the original carrier and telecom circle location[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span).
