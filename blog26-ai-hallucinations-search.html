Blog 26: AI Hallucinations in Search — How AI Can Sometimes Give Incorrect Information

September 2026 · Technical Web Research · Mobile No Track Technical Research Team

Artificial intelligence is now being used in many search systems to make information easier to understand. Instead of showing only a list of web pages, an AI-powered search feature may provide a short answer or summary at the top of the results.

This can be very useful. A person can ask a question in normal language and receive an answer that is easier to read.

Important: AI can sometimes generate information that is incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or connected to the wrong source.

This type of problem is commonly called an AI hallucination.

The word "hallucination" can sound complicated, but the basic idea is simple. It means that an AI system produces an answer that sounds reasonable or confident but contains information that is not actually correct.

This does not necessarily mean that the entire answer is false. Sometimes only one detail is wrong. Sometimes the information is outdated. In other situations, the information may be correct but associated with the wrong website, product, organization, or source.

Understanding this difference is important because many people naturally assume that an answer shown by an AI-powered search feature must be correct.

It is not always.

What Is an AI Hallucination?

An AI hallucination happens when an artificial intelligence system generates an answer that contains information that does not accurately match the available facts.

"What does this website provide?"

An AI-powered system may use information from search results or other available sources when producing an answer. If information about different websites is similar, there is a possibility that details can be connected incorrectly.

The final answer may look completely normal. But one part of the answer could belong to another website. That is an example of a source or attribution error.

Another simple example would be asking an AI about a product that has recently changed its features. If older information is used, the answer may describe a feature that no longer exists.

The answer may sound convincing because it is written in natural language, but the information can still be wrong.

So an AI hallucination does not necessarily look like obvious nonsense.

The most difficult errors are the ones that look believable.

Why Can AI Give the Wrong Information?

1. Similar information can be difficult to separate

The internet contains millions of websites, pages, names, descriptions and references. Sometimes two websites can have similar names, similar keywords, or similar topics. If information from different sources is presented close together in search results, an AI-generated summary can potentially connect information incorrectly. This is particularly important when a question is about a specific website or organization. The name alone may not always be enough to establish that two sources are the same.

2. Information on the internet changes

Websites change regularly. A page can be updated. A service can change. Policies can change. A tool can receive new features. An old article can remain available even after the information inside it has become outdated. If an AI-generated answer relies on information that does not reflect the latest situation, the answer may be incomplete or incorrect. This is why the date of information matters. For important information, users should look at the original page and check whether the information is current.

3. Context can be misunderstood

A sentence can have different meanings depending on the surrounding information. For example, one page may describe what a service does, while another page may discuss a completely different service with a similar name. If the surrounding context is not correctly understood, information can potentially be connected to the wrong subject. This is known as a context problem. The individual words may be real. The information may even be real. But the connection between the information and the subject can be wrong.

What Is an AI Overview?

An AI Overview is an AI-generated summary that can appear within search results for certain questions. Instead of requiring the user to open several pages and read everything, the system may provide a summarized response. This can save time and make complicated subjects easier to understand. But a summary is still a summary. It should not automatically be treated as an unquestionable source of truth. AI-generated search answers can contain mistakes, and important information should be checked against the original sources.

This is especially important when the question involves:

  • Money
  • Legal information
  • Health information
  • Security
  • Privacy
  • Account settings
  • Technical instructions
  • Current policies
  • Current prices
  • Current services

How Can Source Attribution Become Wrong?

Source attribution simply means connecting information to the source it came from. Suppose there are two different websites: Website A and Website B. Both have similar names and both discuss similar subjects. Website A provides one service. Website B provides another service. An AI-generated answer could potentially take information associated with Website B and present it while answering a question about Website A. The information itself might not be completely made up. The problem is that it has been connected to the wrong source.

"The information is false."

and

"The information may be real, but it is being attributed to the wrong source."

Why Similar Website Names Can Cause Confusion

Domain names are one of the easiest ways to distinguish websites, but ordinary users often remember only the main name. For example, two unrelated websites could have names that look almost identical while having completely different domain names. A person may see a short name in an AI-generated answer and assume it refers to the website they were asking about. That assumption can be wrong. The safest way to distinguish websites is to check the actual domain name.

"Which exact domain is being referenced?"

What Is a Search Snippet?

When a website appears in search results, users may see a short piece of text describing the page. This is commonly called a search snippet. A snippet helps users understand what a page may contain before opening it. However, a snippet is not the same thing as reading the complete webpage. A short piece of text can leave out important context. For example, a page might say: "This information applies only to..." If the surrounding sentence is not visible, someone could misunderstand what the statement means. AI-generated summaries can have a similar challenge because information is condensed into a shorter explanation.

AI Hallucination Does Not Always Mean Completely Fake Information

Completely Incorrect Information

The answer gives a fact that is simply not true.

Partially Incorrect Information

Most of the answer is correct, but one or two details are wrong.

Outdated Information

The answer was once correct but is no longer current.

Missing Information

The answer leaves out an important detail that changes the meaning.

Wrong Attribution

The information may be real, but it is connected to the wrong website or source.

Mixed Information

Information from different sources can potentially be combined in a way that creates an incorrect overall answer.

Why Does an AI Answer Sometimes Look So Convincing?

Modern AI systems are designed to communicate in natural language. They can produce answers that are: Well structured, Grammatically correct, Detailed, Confident, Easy to understand. But good writing does not automatically mean correct information.

Confidence in the wording is not proof of accuracy.

How Can a Normal User Check an AI Answer?

Check the Actual Website

If an AI answer mentions a website, open the website and check the information directly.

Check the Domain

Look carefully at the domain name. Small differences can identify completely different websites.

Check the Date

If the information is about a current service, policy, price or feature, check when the information was published or updated.

Open Supporting Sources

If an AI-generated answer provides links or references, open them. Do not assume that the summary says exactly what the source says.

Compare Important Information

For an important decision, check more than one reliable source.

Look for Context

Read enough of the original page to understand what the statement actually means.

What Should You Do If an AI Answer Looks Wrong?

First, don't immediately assume that everything in the answer is false. Identify the specific claim that looks incorrect. Then check the original source. If the source says something different, compare the two. You can also perform a normal web search for the same question and compare the results. Many search systems provide a way for users to report an inaccurate or unhelpful AI-generated answer. Using those reporting options can help identify problems in search results. Most importantly, do not forward an unverified AI answer as a confirmed fact. This is especially important when the information could affect another person, business, website, payment, account, privacy or security.

AI Search Is a Tool, Not a Replacement for Verification

AI-powered search can be extremely useful. It can help people understand complicated subjects, find relevant information and save time. But it should be treated as an information-assistance tool rather than an unquestionable authority.

AI answer → open source → check context → verify important facts.

This takes only a little extra time. It also helps separate genuine information from mistakes.

A Simple Example

Imagine a user searches for information about a particular website. The AI-generated answer provides three statements. The first statement is correct. The second statement is from an older page. The third statement belongs to another website with a similar name. The answer may still look perfectly normal. If the user reads only the AI summary, they may believe all three statements describe the same website. But after checking the original sources, the difference becomes clear. This example shows why source identity and context are just as important as the information itself.

Why This Matters for Website Owners Too

AI-generated search errors are not only a problem for users. They can also affect website owners. A website may publish accurate information, but an automated system could potentially misunderstand or incorrectly associate that information with another source. If a user's first impression comes from an incorrect summary, the website owner may receive an inaccurate representation. This is why clear website structure is useful. A website should clearly identify: What it provides, Who operates the site, What its services do, What its limitations are, When information was updated, Where important information comes from. Clear information makes it easier for both people and automated systems to understand the subject correctly.

The Difference Between AI Error and Human Error

AI is not the only source of incorrect information. Humans can also misunderstand webpages, copy outdated information, confuse names, or report facts incorrectly. The difference is that AI-generated answers can be produced quickly and shown to many users. Therefore, a single incorrect generated statement can potentially spread much faster than an ordinary individual mistake. This is another reason why verification remains important.

A Simple Rule for Everyone

You don't need to become an AI expert. Just remember these five rules:

  1. Don't believe an AI answer only because it sounds confident.
  2. Check the actual website when the source matters.
  3. Check the exact domain when names look similar.
  4. Check dates when information can change.
  5. Verify important information before acting on it.

Final Takeaway

Artificial intelligence has made online searching faster and easier, but it is not perfect. An AI-generated search answer can sometimes contain incorrect information, missing context, outdated details, or incorrect source attribution. A particularly confusing situation can occur when different websites have similar names or when information from multiple sources is combined into a short answer. The important thing is not to panic or assume that every AI answer is false. Instead, understand how to verify it. Check the original website. Check the exact domain. Check the date. Read the surrounding context. Compare important information with reliable sources.

An AI-generated answer is a convenient starting point, not a guarantee that every statement is correct.

Using AI responsibly means taking advantage of its speed while still using human judgment to verify important facts. That balance is a practical and safer way to use AI-powered search.

Key Facts

  • AI systems can sometimes generate incorrect information.
  • AI-generated answers can contain partially correct information as well as errors.
  • An answer can be wrong because of outdated information or missing context.
  • Similar names and domains can potentially create source-attribution confusion.
  • Real information can still be misleading if it is connected to the wrong source.
  • A confident writing style does not prove that an answer is factually correct.
  • Important information should be checked against original and reliable sources.
  • Checking the exact domain is useful when different websites have similar names.
  • Users should not treat AI-generated summaries as a substitute for verification.
  • Human judgment remains important when using AI-powered search.
